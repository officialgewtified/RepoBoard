How to post (about a minute)

  1. Download the video, then open it from the download list (or the Files app) and tap Share, then Save Video so it lands in Photos.
  2. Check the fact sheet: every number in the video with a link to its source. Launch times slip, so glance at those the morning you post.
  3. Copy caption on this page.
  4. TikTok: + → Upload → pick the video → Next → paste the caption → Post. You can add a trending sound at low volume in the editor; keep the voiceover louder.
  5. YouTube Shorts: + → Create a Short → Upload → pick the video → use the caption's first line as the title → Upload.
  6. Cover image: every video now has one (big number, yellow label). Use it as the TikTok cover (Edit cover, then upload it from Photos if your app offers that) and as the YouTube thumbnail on the long-form.
  7. Post the same video to both. Mornings work, and so does early evening, when people plan to look up tonight.

Data: NASA JPL, NOAA SWPC, The Space Devs. Footage: NASA (credited in every caption; never use NASA logos as the channel's branding). If something ever looks off, skip that day rather than post it.
