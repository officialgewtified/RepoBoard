How to post (about a minute)
  1. Check the fact sheet: every number with a link to its source. Launch times slip, so glance at those the morning you post.
  2. Download the video, open it from your downloads and tap Share, then Save Video.
  3. Tap Copy title and Copy caption, and download the Cover image.
  4. Post to TikTok and YouTube Shorts, then tap Mark posted. Quick hits work any time; the full report does best in the morning or early evening.

Data: NASA JPL, NOAA SWPC, The Space Devs, USNO. Footage: NASA (credited in every caption; never use NASA logos as the channel's branding).
