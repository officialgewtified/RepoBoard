How to post (about a minute)
  1. Download the video, open it from your downloads (or Files) and tap Share, then Save Video.
  2. Tap Copy title and Copy caption, and download the Cover image.
  3. TikTok: + → Upload → pick the video → paste the caption → set the cover → Post.
  4. YouTube: Shorts for the vertical ones (paste the title), a regular upload for the long-form (title, caption, cover image as the thumbnail).
  5. Tap Mark posted so you know what's done. Mornings (7 to 9am ET) catch people checking last night's scores.

Everything comes straight from the official box scores. If something ever looks off, skip that day rather than post it.
